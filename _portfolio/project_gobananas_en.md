Made for [[Unity 20th Anniversary Game Jam]](https://itch.io/jam/unity-20th-anniversary-game-jam) (Theme: "Timeless")

A celebration of timelessness in every sense:

- Built on the timeless clicker genre that has captivated players for generations
- Your ultimate goal? Create an eternal DIAMOND BANANA - a symbol of something truly imperishable

Click, collect, and craft your way to banana paradise. Each click brings you closer to forging the ultimate treasure: a banana that will last forever.

Go bananas and make something truly timeless! 🍌💎

{:.list-inline}
- Release Date: November 2025
- Category: Game
- Platform: [[WebGL]](https://lab21.itch.io/go-bananas)
